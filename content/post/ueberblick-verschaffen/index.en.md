The author received about 500 slides together with the projectors. How do you get an overview of them? How do you find the right motifs? Unfortunately there is only one way: look at all of them…

A setup made of a webcam and a projector lends itself to this, see below.
{{< figure src="59571804977_0.jpg" alt="Projector" >}}

The next step is then to manage and tag the images in a suitable program.

The first choice was supposed to be Picasa — the Windows version also offers capture via webcam. Unfortunately it was not possible to make this version work with the webcam under [Wine](http://www.winehq.org/ "Wine") or [VirtualBox](https://www.virtualbox.org/ "VirtualBox"). Under VirtualBox, a five-year-old [bug](https://www.virtualbox.org/ticket/242 "VirtualBox bug") stood in the way.

In the end a combination of Picasa and Photo Booth remained, with Picasa monitoring the output directory of Photo Booth.
